# ⚔️ *Youjo Senki – Saga of Tanya the Evil*

> “Being powerless is a sin.” – Tanya Degurechaff  

---

## 🪖 Overview  

*Youjo Senki* follows **Tanya Degurechaff**, a ruthless soldier reborn in a world of endless war.  
Armed with intelligence, magic, and pure ambition, she rises through the ranks of the Imperial Army — crushing enemies with military precision and cold logic.  

---

## 💣 Themes  

- **War and Strategy** – every battle is a test of power, discipline, and survival.  
- **Faith vs. Reason** – Tanya defies God (*Being X*) and believes only in human will.  
- **Order through Fear** – she rules with efficiency and intimidation.
